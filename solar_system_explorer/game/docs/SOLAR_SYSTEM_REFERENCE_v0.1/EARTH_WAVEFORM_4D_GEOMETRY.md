## How 4D Spheres Work

### S² (the normal sphere)

Every point on S² is described by 2 angles: (θ, φ). The surface is 2-dimensional. Total area = 4πR².

Every point on S³ is described by 3 angles: (χ, θ, φ) where χ ∈ [0, π]. The surface is 3-dimensional. Total volume = 2π²R³.

In Cartesian 4D coordinates:
```
x₁ = R cos(χ)
x₂ = R sin(χ) cos(θ)
x₃ = R sin(χ) sin(θ) cos(φ)
x₄ = R sin(χ) sin(θ) sin(φ)
```

5. **Hopf fibration.** S³ decomposes as a circle bundle over S².
