Three different source directions. Three different physical characters. ʻOumuamua dry and anomalously accelerating, aspect ratio possibly 10:1, no coma. These are not a population sample. Three is not a sample. But three is more than zero, and each adds a constraint.

It passed Mars at 0.194 AU and Jupiter at 0.358 AU with essentially no orbital change. It entered. It turned slightly. It exited. The entire transit through the inner system — entry to current position — was less than one year.

It is now in Gemini, 4.6 AU from Sol, moving at 61 km/s, and nearly at the same declination (+20°) as M44. I have no interpretation to offer. That is what the data says.
