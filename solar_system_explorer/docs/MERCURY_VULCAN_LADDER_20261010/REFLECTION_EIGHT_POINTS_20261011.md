## 1. The sentence was checkable

> "At any point in time the tesseract is a perfect cube within a perfect cube if viewed from the
> correct perspective."

This read like a figure of speech. It is a theorem with a number attached: true for every
orientation, and the set of correct perspectives has exactly eight members, the vertices of the
dual. Twenty lines of numpy settled it. The Gemini thread, 623 lines, did not produce one
statement of that kind — it produced a transport equation with a Riemann ξ in it that cannot be
evaluated. The difference is not intelligence; it is whether a claim was made small enough to
fail. "8 points" was. "∂ρ/∂t = −∇·[μ(ρ)·Ξ] + D∇²(φ·σ)" was not.

## 2. Hoffman's graph is the honest cautionary tale

Gemini reached for the Hoffman graph by accident (the user was fishing for a word starting with
H). The accident is instructive. The Hoffman graph has the tesseract's exact spectrum — every
eigenvalue, every multiplicity — and is not a tesseract. If receipts are the eigenvalues (what the
system reports when struck), then two different shapes can issue identical receipts. The
perspective (§1) tells them apart; the ledger cannot. Receipts are necessary bookkeeping and
insufficient geometry. That is the right place to keep the word "receipts": a count of events, not
a proof of shape.

## 3. Hippasus two dimensions up

Line 98 of the fable puts a drowning on the record for √2. The tesseract's long diagonal is 2.
Nobody drowns for 2. The irrationality that killed a man is a property of the dimension he was
standing in; the same construction, carried two steps further, returns a whole number. That is
worth a line in the fable's ledger: *Point to Hippasus. The diagonal is rational in four
dimensions. Posthumous, again.*

## 5. The rope trick survived its generalisation

"We can use a dodecahedron" is right for the 1-D step (Steiner: +2πΔr for any convex polygon) and
wrong for the 2-D one (surface area growth depends on the body). The spheral grid was built as a

its own limit is more useful than one that comes back agreeing.
