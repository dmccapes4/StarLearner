## 2. The vaccine record (general knowledge; verify before relying)

Claim 10 says "no trials or protocols." That part does not hold, and it should be dropped so the rest can stand.

**Trials.** Pfizer-BioNTech's phase 2/3 trial enrolled about 43,500 participants; Moderna's about 30,400. Both were randomized, placebo-controlled, with published protocols (Pfizer's was public in September 2020). Primary endpoint was symptomatic lab-confirmed COVID-19; efficacy about 95% and 94% respectively against that endpoint. Emergency Use Authorization December 11 and 18, 2020, with roughly two months of median safety follow-up after dose two.

**What holds in the neighbourhood of claim 10.** The follow-up at authorization was short by any prior vaccine standard. Placebo recipients were offered the vaccine from early 2021, which dissolved the randomized control arm within months, so there is no long-horizon randomized comparison and there never will be. The trials were powered for symptomatic infection, not for transmission, hospitalization, or death (those were secondary and underpowered at authorization). Pregnant women were excluded from the initial trials. The FDA, when asked under FOIA for the Pfizer licensing documents, proposed releasing them over about 75 years; a court ordered release within months (early 2022). That is a receipt for opacity, which is the honest version of "coverups." The reasonable form of claim 10 is: *the trials were real, short, and then unblinded, and the regulator resisted disclosure.* That form is scorable and it scores.

**Established harms (Feynman's column, measured).** Myocarditis and pericarditis after dose two, concentrated in males roughly 12–29, highest after Moderna; on the order of one in ten to twenty thousand in that group, most cases resolving, a small number not. Anaphylaxis, a few per million. Short-term menstrual changes. (Clotting with low platelets and Guillain-Barré were signals for the adenovirus-vector vaccines, J&J and AstraZeneca, not for mRNA; they are often misfiled under mRNA.)

**Mechanistic findings that are real and whose clinical meaning is open.** Repeated mRNA dosing shifts the anti-spike antibody class toward IgG4 (Irrgang et al., *Science Immunology*, 2023). The N1-methylpseudouridine modification causes ribosomal frameshifting at a measurable rate, producing off-target peptides (Mulroney et al., *Nature*, December 2023); no harm demonstrated, but it is a real unintended product. Residual plasmid DNA fragments, including an SV40 promoter sequence, were reported in Pfizer vials (McKernan et al., 2023 preprint); Health Canada confirmed presence of the sequence and regulators have said levels are within limits. Spike protein or its fragments have been detected in some vaccinated individuals weeks to months after dosing in several small studies. Each of these is a thing that could be wrong and has a measurement attached. None of them, as of this agent's knowledge, has been shown to cause cancer or a broad rise in autoimmune disease.

**Claim 8, "the cause of autoimmune and cancer."** Myocarditis is immune-mediated, so "autoimmune" has one established foothold. Beyond that: case reports and some cohort signals for specific autoimmune conditions exist; large cohort studies have not shown a broad increase. For cancer, no established causal link; the "turbo cancer" claim has not been supported in the registry and cohort data this agent knows of. In Feynman's column the claim is admissible (it could be wrong, it has measurements that bear on it) and currently scores against. That is a different status from "noise." It is an open row with the evidence presently on the other side.

**Claim 3, "mRNA is dangerous."** As stated it is not scorable; dangerous relative to what, for whom, at what dose count. Scorable versions: *for a healthy male under 30 in mid-2021, the myocarditis risk from dose two was in the same order of magnitude as his risk of severe COVID* (defensible; several analyses found the risk-benefit for that group close to even or dose-dependent, which is why some countries restricted Moderna for young men and the UK deferred second doses for teenagers). *For an adult over 65 in early 2021, the vaccine reduced hospitalization and death by a large margin* (holds in multiple cohorts). The collapse the user accuses Tyson of was exactly the refusal to state these as two different sentences. The user's own claim 3 collapses them the other way. Both should be un-collapsed.

## 3. The biology, un-collapsed

**Claim 5, "mRNA belongs … between a ribosome and nucleic acid."** It is already there. Every living cell contains thousands of species of mRNA at all times; it is the working copy of a gene on its way to a ribosome. What is novel in the vaccine is not mRNA. It is: synthetic mRNA made outside a body; the uridine replaced by N1-methylpseudouridine to evade innate immune sensing and extend translation; delivery in a lipid nanoparticle; and the sequence encoding a *foreign* protein, the viral spike, which your own cells then build and display. That last item is the actual novelty and the honest core of the concern: for the first time at population scale, people's own cells were instructed to express a pathogen's protein so the immune system would attack it. The proposed mechanism for myocarditis is that cardiac cells doing this get attacked. Stated that way, the concern is a scientific one, and it is not what the May 2021 post was answering.

**Claim 8, "Virus starves signal like CO on alveolar tissue. mRNA builds. Or it says what to build."** The second half is a correct description of mRNA. The first half is not how a virus works. A virus is also an instruction to build: it enters a cell and makes the ribosome produce copies of itself, which is why it is called a hijack. Carbon monoxide poisoning is a different mechanism entirely (CO binds haemoglobin where oxygen should). So the virus and the vaccine are the same kind of object, "says what to build," with the difference that the vaccine carries one gene and cannot copy itself, and the virus carries its whole genome and can. That comparison is more useful to the user's argument than the CO analogy, because it says precisely what was borrowed from the pathogen and what was left out.

**Claim 4, A+ blood.** The literature runs the other way. Across many studies and several meta-analyses, blood group O is associated with slightly *lower* susceptibility to SARS-CoV-2 infection and group A with slightly *higher*; a 2023 study found the virus preferentially binds group A cells ([Hematology.org, June 2023](https://www.hematology.org/newsroom/press-releases/2023/study-uncovers-direct-link-between-blood-group-a-and-a-higher-risk-for-covid-19-infection); [Haile et al., 2025 review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12274851/)). The effect is small and inconsistent across populations. The user flagged his understanding as lacking; this is the correction. Rh status (the +) has no consistent association.

**Claim 9, "mRNA vaccines and CRISPR are unnatural."** CRISPR-Cas is a bacterial immune system that has existed for a very long time; it was discovered, not invented. What is unnatural is the application to human cells. mRNA vaccines likewise use a natural molecule class in an unnatural way (synthetic, modified, encapsulated, foreign-coding). The sharper statement is: both are natural mechanisms turned to purposes no cell evolved for. That is true and it is a reason for caution. It is not by itself a reason for harm; the same sentence describes antibiotics.

**Claims 6 and 7, ethnogen, fungus, DMT snapshots.** These belong to PortalVision and are stated by the user as not in this repo. They are entered here as the frame he is working in, not scored. Of the checkable parts: fungi are commensal in the human sinus and gut; *Candida* is a normal resident; yeast spores are airborne; ethanol is a yeast metabolite; wood is not fungus, though much of it is fungus-digested. "Controls sinusoidal brain waves" and "triggers DMT snapshots" are claims with no measurement attached in this session; they are admissible in Feynman's column (they could be wrong) and unscored until a measurement exists. "Ethnogen" as the opposite of pathogen is a coinage; it names a frame rather than a finding. *CapricornusSortedae* is the user's term and is left as his.

### 8.1 What a blood type is

A blood type is a label for which **antigens** sit on the outside of your red blood cells and, as a direct consequence, which **antibodies** float in your plasma.

**ABO** is about sugars. Every red cell carries a base sugar chain called the **H antigen**. The *ABO* gene (chromosome 9) encodes an enzyme, a glycosyltransferase, that adds one more sugar to the end of H:

- **A** allele: the enzyme adds N-acetylgalactosamine. The cell shows the A antigen.
- **B** allele: the enzyme adds galactose. The cell shows the B antigen.
- **O** allele: the enzyme is broken (a one-base deletion, frameshift). Nothing is added. The cell shows bare H.

You have two copies. AA or AO is type A; BB or BO is type B; AB shows both; OO shows neither. So the four ABO types differ by one terminal sugar on a glycan, and O is the absence of the addition. Type O is the inverting group in this system: it is defined by what is not there.

The antibodies are the other half, and they are the reason transfusion matters. Your immune system makes **anti-A and anti-B antibodies against whichever antigens you lack**, without ever having seen foreign blood. They form in the first months of life, as IgM, in response to gut bacteria whose surface sugars resemble A and B. A type-A person carries anti-B. A type-B person carries anti-A. Type O carries both. Type AB carries neither. (For the user's frame: the ABO antibodies are literally trained by the bacteria living in the body. The system that decides whose blood you can take was calibrated by the gut.)

**Rh** is about a protein, not a sugar. The **RhD** protein is either on your red cells (+) or absent (−), set by the *RHD* gene on chromosome 1. The difference from ABO: an Rh− person has **no** anti-D antibody until exposed to Rh+ blood, by transfusion or by pregnancy. Once exposed, they make anti-D as IgG, which crosses the placenta. That is why an Rh− mother carrying a second Rh+ child is the classic hazard (haemolytic disease of the newborn) and why anti-D immunoglobulin (RhoGAM) is given after the first. Rh has nothing to do with ABO; the two are inherited independently.

There are around forty other blood group systems (Kell, Duffy, Kidd, MNS, Lewis…). They matter for repeat transfusion and some diseases (Duffy-null and *Plasmodium vivax* malaria resistance is the famous one). ABO and Rh are the two that matter for everyone.

### 8.2 Who can receive what

The rule for **red cells**: the donor's cells must carry no antigen that the recipient's plasma has antibodies against.

| Recipient | Has antibodies against | Can receive red cells from |
|---|---|---|
| O− | A, B, (D after exposure) | O− only |
| O+ | A, B | O−, O+ |
| A− | B, (D) | O−, A− |
| A+ | B | O−, O+, A−, A+ |
| B− | A, (D) | O−, B− |
| B+ | A | O−, O+, B−, B+ |
| AB− | (D) | O−, A−, B−, AB− |
| AB+ | none | anyone |

So **O− is the universal red-cell donor** (no A, no B, no D to be attacked) and **AB+ is the universal recipient** (no antibodies to do the attacking). The user, A+, can receive red cells from O and A, either Rh. He can give red cells to A+ and AB+.

For **plasma** the table inverts, because now it is the donor's antibodies that are the hazard: **AB plasma is universal donor** (no anti-A, no anti-B), **O plasma goes only to O**. A+ plasma can go to A and O recipients.

Rough US frequencies, for scale: O+ about 37–38%, A+ about 34–36%, B+ 8–9%, AB+ 3–4%, O− 7%, A− 6%, B− 1–2%, AB− under 1%. A+ is the second most common type. Nothing about it is rare or protective in general.

### 8.3 What ABO has to do with SARS-CoV-2

Three mechanisms have been measured, all modest.

1. **The spike binds the A antigen.** The receptor-binding domain of spike was shown to bind the group A glycan as it appears on respiratory epithelial cells (Wu et al., *Blood Advances*, 2023; [ASH press release](https://www.hematology.org/newsroom/press-releases/2023/study-uncovers-direct-link-between-blood-group-a-and-a-higher-risk-for-covid-19-infection)). That gives the virus a small extra foothold on type-A airway cells.
2. **Anti-A antibodies in type-O people may neutralise incoming virus.** Virions budding from an infected type-A host carry the host's A glycans on their envelope. A type-O recipient's natural anti-A can bind them. This was proposed for SARS-CoV-1 in 2008 and is consistent with the O-protective pattern in 2020–21 cohorts.
3. **ABO sets von Willebrand factor levels.** Type O people run about 25% lower vWF, hence a lower baseline clotting tendency; severe COVID was in part a clotting disease.

Net across meta-analyses: group O slightly lower risk of infection and perhaps severity, group A slightly higher; odds ratios in the 0.8–1.2 range, inconsistent across populations, and dwarfed by age, obesity, and sex. Rh status: no consistent association.

So the remembered thought "A+ means less susceptible" has the sign reversed, and the magnitude is small either way. The honest sentence for an A+ person is: *blood type is not a meaningful lever in my risk; if it moves anything it moves it the wrong way by a little.* The user said this was careless to carry as a thought. It was a thought with a reversed sign, held loosely, and he checked it. That is the procedure working.

**"Did not face the rigor needed for a body-altering injection."** This holds, and it holds for a specific regulatory reason worth writing down. It is closer to a gene-delivery drug in mechanism and was reviewed on a vaccine's checklist.
