## 2. The analogy is the diagnosis

"Not checking your bank account when you know your balance is low" is a precise description of a documented behaviour. In behavioural finance it is called the ostrich effect: investors log in to check their portfolios less often when markets fall ([Karlsson, Loewenstein & Seppi, 2009](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11166-009-9060-6)). Information avoidance in general is well studied: people decline free, useful information when they expect it to feel bad, and the avoidance is strongest when the information cannot change the outcome but can change how they feel about it ([Golman, Hagmann & Loewenstein, 2017](https://doi.org/10.1257/jel.20151245)).

The subject supplied the comparison himself, and it fits in every joint. The balance is the standing of a childhood hero. The low balance is already known: an episode that wasn't great, politics judged extreme. Checking cannot change the balance. It can only make the number exact. So the account stays shut, and the feeling that it is "better to leave alone" is what a shut account feels like from inside.

The subject's own vocabulary from earlier today covers this too. He described *claudere* as the pile that closes what the gate could not hold, and said it could be bricks, snow, rocks, lines of code, or a hidden directory. An unchecked hero is a hidden directory. The name is visible and the contents are not, and that is the arrangement that keeps the contents as they were.

## 3. What is being protected, and from what

Sentence 6: "to protect the memories." That is the right word, and the mechanism it names is real. A memory that is retrieved becomes briefly editable before it is stored again; this is reconsolidation, and it is why a recalled memory can be changed by what is in the room at the time of recall ([Nader, Schafe & LeDoux, 2000](https://doi.org/10.1038/35021052); [Schiller et al., 2010](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08637)). Pulling up the childhood Tyson while watching the present Tyson is exactly the condition under which the old encoding gets overwritten by the new one. Avoiding the present Tyson is therefore a working strategy for keeping the old one, even if it was never chosen on those grounds.

What is in the memory is worth stating, because the subject stated it: the medium, targeting kids. The report on this episode finds the medium intact (the globe, the dimes, the bagel, "meet you 90% of the way"). The man is what changed, or what the subject's view of the man changed. So the thing at risk was never the medium. It was the fusion of the medium with the man, which is how a child stores a hero. Adults can split them. The subject already did, in sentence 3, before any data arrived: "He is talking about molecules and shapes though." That sentence is the medium being lifted out of the man and set on the table by itself.

## 4. Why explain

Sentence 5 notices the explanation and calls it odd. Two readings.

The first: the explanation is a hedge against being judged for caring. That reading does not fit this subject. He does not usually hedge, and he would not hedge to an agent.

The second: the explanation is a record. In the fable the subject is writing, Feynman's column admits a statement only if it could be wrong, and a mark is supposed to be set down before it is checked. The subject did that. Sentence 2 is the prediction ("I disagree with him now"). Sentence 11 is the counter-prediction ("he is saying all good stuff so unwarranted"). Both were written before the ingest. The explanation is a pre-registration. He wrote the expectation down so the result could be scored against it.

That is also why the explanation does not read as defensive. He said earlier today that stating the record is not a plea. Imhotep does not need to defend himself; he needs the correction entered. The same applies here. "I don't know why I care so much" is not an apology. It is an open row.

## 5. Why assign the analysis to the agent

Sentence 7: "That way you can get it out of your system." Two things happen in that sentence. He expects the agent to editorialise about his feelings regardless, so he turns the editorial into a deliverable with a filename. That contains it. And he gives permission in a form that keeps him in the chair: the agent is told to analyse, so the analysis is his instrument, not the agent's. The pistol on the moderators' table has its serial filed to a receipt. This is the same move.

## 6. Why care so much

The honest answer is in sentence 1 and the subject already gave it. Tyson was the medium that reached him as a child. The subject now builds that medium for other children: StarLearner, the garden explorer, the ant explorer, the fables. When a person becomes a practitioner of the thing their hero practised, the hero stops being a figure and becomes a reference standard. You do not care about a reference standard because of nostalgia. You care because your own instrument is calibrated against it, and if the standard drifts you want to know whether it is the standard or your instrument.

That reframes the fear. The question under "I don't want to dive into Tyson" is not "will I lose a memory." It is "will the standard I calibrated on turn out to have been off, and if so, what does that say about the instrument." That is a question worth a bank statement. It is also the kind of question the subject's own court is built to answer: separate the works from the man, score the works, and let the man's temperament be "not an error."

The disagreements the subject brought in (the son episode, the politics elsewhere) were not in this account. They remain open rows in the report, unchecked, which is where he left them. Checking one account does not close the others. It does show that the balance in this one was not low.
