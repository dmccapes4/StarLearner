STRUCTURAL FACTS ABOUT star_pipe (hold for every input)
  T1  F = L + |L−B| + B = 2·max(L,B).  F is always even; an F-group is 'same max digit-sum'. F=14 <=> max(L,B)=7.
      (prior report's 'self-pipe stars have even F' is true but weaker: ALL F are even.)
  T2  G = leading digit of round(λ). For λ in 100–199, G=1 regardless. Gemini/Cancer/Leo all collapse to G=1; G_diff=0 there carries no information.
  T3  L and B are digit sums of ROUNDED degrees; a star 0.5° off changes L by up to 9. The pipe is a coarse hash of position, not a geometric property.
  T4  Self-pipe <=> digit-sum(round λ) ≡ digit-sum(round |β|) (mod 10). Expected rate ≈ 1/10 under independence; observed 7/49.
