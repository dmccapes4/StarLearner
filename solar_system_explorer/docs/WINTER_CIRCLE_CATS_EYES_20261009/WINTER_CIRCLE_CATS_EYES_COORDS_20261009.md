## The Cat Geometry

**Procyon + Gomeisa = eyes.** The cat faces roughly ESE (toward Sirius, its prey).

**The hunched back = Pollux → Castor → Capella arc:**
```
                    Capella (+46°)        ← apex of arch / spine peak
                   /
         Castor (+32°)
        /
Pollux (+28°) — — — — — — (Procyon +5°)  ← haunches to eye level
                                |
                              Gomeisa (+8°) [left eye]
                              Procyon (+5°) [right eye]
                                |
                              Sirius (−17°)  ← what the cat is staring at
```

The arc Pollux → Castor → Capella rises ~18° in Dec over ~3h in RA. That's the hunched back — the spine arching upward from the haunches toward the neck, cat crouched and watching Sirius.

Aldebaran (+16°, 4h 35m) sits to the West — the right hip / rear quarter.
Rigel (−8°, 5h 14m) is the front left paw, extended.
Betelgeuse (+7°, 5h 55m) is roughly the throat/chest.

**Orion's belt** hangs directly below Betelgeuse: the collar or chest markings.

## Notes for StarLearner / GalacticView

- The "Winter Circle" framing (hexagon) is a modern Western asterism
- The cat reading is a geometric re-frame: two bright eyes (CMi pair) + arched spine + prey (Sirius)
- Gomeisa (β CMi) is rarely named in popular astronomy — the cat reading makes it essential
- Capella is the highest point of the arch; it transits near zenith from mid-northern latitudes
- Sirius is the dominant gravity of the whole scene: everything faces toward it
