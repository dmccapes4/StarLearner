# Note: Retrograde simulations (future)

**Status.** Not built yet — capture the idea so Zodiac Sky / Free Flight can grow into it.

## Why it’s cool

From Earth, outer planets sometimes appear to loop backward against the stars (**apparent retrograde motion**). It’s a perspective effect from Earth overtaking them on the inside track — not the planet literally reversing.

Kids already love “weird sky tricks.” A short sim would nail two educational beats:

1. **Astronomy:** relative orbits, not magic.
2. **Culture:** why ancient sky-watchers (and modern astrology) talk about “Mercury retrograde.”

## Preferred camera (updated)

**Rise up and look down at a portion of the orrery** — not a locked night-sky starfield alone.

| Beat | What the player sees |
|------|----------------------|
| Pick a planet (Mercury / Mars first) | Brief cue near Earth |
| Rise | Camera climbs above the ecliptic; a slice of the orrery (Sun + Earth + target + a few neighbors) fills the view |
| Play loop | Time speeds; Earth’s faster inside track overtakes; from the high view the target’s sky-path loops, while the orrery still shows true forward orbits |
| Optional astrology beat | One honest line: “Astrologers named that loop *retrograde* — people still talk about it today” |

The high-orrery framing makes the *cause* readable (who’s catching whom) while a secondary inset or VO can still say “this is what it looks like from Earth.”

Reuse Free Flight / Zodiac tap verbs: pick target → watch. No new motion controls.

## Honest framing

- Prefer **outer planets + Mercury** (classic loops).
- Label the view **“from Earth / above the orrery”** so we don’t imply geocentrism as physics.
- Keep astrology as **story / culture**, astronomy as **cause**.

## Hooks already in the product

- Free Flight: Earth arrival tiles → **Constellations** / **Astrological signs**; frame ✧ toggle for sky fly-to.
- Zodiac Sky: season cinematic keeps Earth on its orbit, then looks from Earth.
- Later tile or panel button: **“Why planets loop”** → short retrograde theatre (rise + orrery slice), then back.
