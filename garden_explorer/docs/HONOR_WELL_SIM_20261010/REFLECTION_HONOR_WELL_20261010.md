## 1. What the model is for

Three documents of his are already in the room and the model joins them.

`On_Narrative_Erosion` §"On Conviction, and Aslama" gives the three levels: **thoughts** on surface perception, **beliefs** on evidentiary threshold, **convictions** on alignment with values. Tonight they got positions in a fluid. Thoughts are ripples on the surface. Beliefs are anchors hanging below a molecule, and they slow it down, which is why abandoning them makes you faster. Convictions are foam: they sit on the surface and are not pulled by the slope, because they are not in the fluid at all. The same section says a conviction is changed only by evidence that "invalidates a person's values … which is ego death." In the model: only a splash takes foam under. That sentence and this mechanic are the same sentence.

The same essay's **path of lower entropic field resistance** is the molecule's rule of motion: not the lowest-resistance path (that would be staying put) but the path that looks least messy from where you stand, which on a deformed surface is downhill toward the well. Parents training for their children and athletes training for competitions are molecules choosing depth over rest.

The Nida quotation in that essay separates **fear, shame and guilt**: fear of being caught, shame as "I'd feel terrible if anyone saw me," guilt as the inner failure "irrespective of whether one is caught or seen." The user's new sentence tonight was that American society is "a faux guilt-based society. Rather it is an honor-based society with an everchanging guilt-based shame code." The model gives each of those three words a part. **Honor** is the ball: where esteem sits. **Shame** is location: outcast is a place on the field that others can see. **Guilt** is the set-point: the allostatic baseline that makes a molecule feel the deficit \(R_i < 0\) with nobody watching. "Ever-changing" is the ball moving and changing radius. The thing he called faux is the claim that the set-point is the primary object; the model has the ball primary and the set-point as what the ball leaves behind in each molecule.

## 2. What the first run gave back

Three things came out of the hello world that were not in the derivation.

**Reach is a property of the crowd, not the ball.** The membrane's decay length \(\lambda = \sqrt{T/\kappa}\) is set by cohesion (how much your standing is coupled to your neighbours') and restoring stiffness (how much you return to your own level). The ball sets depth; the medium sets how far depth is felt. In the run, most of a low-cohesion field never felt the well at all. That has an implication the user will recognise: the thing that changed in the last fifteen years is not that the balls got heavier. It is that \(T\) went up. Everyone is now coupled to everyone, so a well anywhere is felt everywhere, and a splash anywhere travels. The internet raised the medium's tension. The derivation's "bowling ball" and "lawn bowl" are both small next to that.

*Qualified by Phase 1.3 (2026-10-11):* raising \(T\) at fixed weight makes a code felt farther and held shallower; the captured region shrinks and at high enough \(T\) the floor drops under the demand and nobody is inside. So the sentence above is right about displacement and about how far a splash travels, and incomplete about capture: at high cohesion only the heavy balls still hold anyone. That is itself a prediction about the last fifteen years, and a testable one: fewer, heavier codes.

**Broad is shallow, narrow is deep, and "which spreads farther" depends on how deep you have to be.** At equal weight the bowling ball holds more people when the demand is low and nobody when the demand is high; the lawn bowl holds a few deeply. His two-layer observation (an honor code nearly everyone is in, with a shame code underneath that a few are deep in) is two balls of the same weight and different radius, and the model says so without being told.

**Planners fall furthest.** The molecules that ran ahead of the ball reached the greatest depth and so had the highest set-points, and when the ball jumped their reward went most negative. Being belief-oriented and logical in pursuit of conformity pays as long as the ball is predictable, and costs most when it is not. The surface followers, who never got as deep, lost less. The foam lost nothing, until a wave.

## 4. The goat, the loop, and the ledger

He remembered the image. Tartarus was his frame then: every LLM loop followed the **TartarusLoop** pattern, a requisite objective plus a human-in-the-loop `checklist.json` plus a `session.jsonl`. The game plan for this sim is written in that pattern, deliberately, because he said all the Titans help humans and this one should too.

On the goat. His recollection is that he did not assume the goat was killed: a fallen angel would not need flesh, and "there is simply no record of the scapegoat after its release into the wood," so he made the goat a *regal companion*. The record: Leviticus 16:21–22 says the goat is sent away "by the hand of a man appointed" into the wilderness, "and the goat shall bear upon him all their iniquities unto a land not inhabited." No death in the text. The later rabbinic record (Mishnah, Yoma 6:6) describes the Second Temple practice of leading the goat to a cliff and pushing it off. So his leap holds for the source and is contradicted by a later practice note; the companion reading is a reading of the text as written, and he is entitled to it. In the model the scapegoat is a specific mechanic worth building in Phase 2 or 3: a molecule the crowd designates outcast to relieve pressure on the floor. One chair removed by consent of the people on the other chairs. It lowers crowd propulsion for everyone who stays, and it is the cheapest intervention a packed well has. That the goat in his picture is standing beside the explorer and not running from him is the Phase 4 case: foam that walked out carrying the weight and did not dissolve.

On the ledger. His chain tonight (sacrifice as delivery of payment authored into the authority at Giza; claims on land as clans; marriage as genetic dampening; the Scarabs as transporters of pastoral payment; Thebes, blades as payment, the triad) is his lexicon and belongs with the other PortAuthority material. It is not scored here. One element of it is taken into the model as a name: depth is a **payment**. Conformity costs something delivered to the ball; the ball keeps the ledger; the set-point is the receipt you carry, and the receipt depreciates. The gas station is the ledger that never closes.

## 6. Where the model is thin, said plainly

- Depth is one number. His molecules have `beliefs[molecule]` "below z in some pattern shared or otherwise supported." The pattern is not modelled yet; \(b_i\) is a count. The shared structure between molecules' belief stacks (two people anchored to the same thing) is the next real object.
- The field anticipates nothing. Only planners look ahead. His sentence was that the *fluid* rushes toward where the ball is heading; in a passive medium it cannot, so anticipation lives in the agents. If he wants the medium itself to anticipate, that is a different equation (an active medium), and the game plan should say so before Phase 1.
- Foam has no mass and no structure. Convictions in a real crowd aggregate (rafts). Phase 4.
- There is no scapegoat mechanic yet. §4 above.
- The Lindsey Clancy example he gave for deep swimmers (people abandoning prior beliefs to move fast with a new current) is recorded as his topical instance and is outside what the model can evaluate; the model can only say that molecules with no anchors arrive first and leave first.
