The Circle of Diamonds and Tesseracts

A fable, with scorekeepers. Richard Feynman (epistemic) and John McClane (operational) moderate a circle drawn in sand: Pythagoras, Archimedes, Plato, Heron, Marconi and Tesla (who will not sit next to each other), Copernicus (subject to change), Imhotep, a late Kant, and something found in a chair. Question on the floor: diamonds, tesseracts, whether up is down, and how to tell without knowing where is what.

Read as written, one voice, 37½ minutes. Speaker labels are spoken because there is no voice change to carry them. Score tables are read row by row. Einstein: no points. Not invited.
