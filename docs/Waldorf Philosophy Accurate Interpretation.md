# Waldorf Philosophy Accurate Interpretation

I researched Waldorf philosophy, its founder Rudolf Steiner, and Stonebridge curriculum. My findings largely align with Waldorf practices with two major exceptions being the pre-2nd grade symbol prohibition and the reductive classification of all electronics as shallow and commercial. The example I use in this interpretation is the comparison between Frances's "ant tunnels" gift and her Ant Explorer gift from me. This contrast between these gifts provides excellent insight into Steiner's intent, the inability of Waldorf Societies to apply his intent in modern age, and the "ultimate sin" of Waldorf which is to view and judge the "outer shell" without striving to understand the underlying mechanics which comprise the magical beauty of nature.

## On Symbol Prohibition, Enamel, and Epigenetics

The prohibition on symbols before age seven is based on a false scientific claim that adult teeth protruding signals that the heart, lungs and liver have completed growing. The logic behind it is that enamel of adult teeth is the most mineral-dense material in the human body therefore when the child's body is able to push them out it represents a spiritual metamorphosis. 

Meanwhile, Steiner's claim that this metamorphosis is also when the child's soul (incorrectly referred to as Ego) "casts off its parental genetic blueprint" is almost a strikingly prophetic statement about the massive epigenetic shift that occurs at this age. However, I do not believe it was Steiner's intent to prophesy epigenetics, and the shift is certainly not connected to enamel mineral density and children's adult teeth protruding.

Waldorf Societies were quick to walk back Steiner's extreme racism as a property of his time yet fail to recognize the clear logical fallacy of this prohibition and make reasonable change. Rather, "M" is taught with an image of a mountain at Stonebridge which is an absurd accommodation of the absurd prohibition. The alternative is to allow symbols when appropriate with a prohibition on worksheets and repetition.

## On Neurology, Rote Metrics, and Bilateral Learning

The real thing that must be avoided is teaching symbols by pattern recognition through repetition to achieve rote metrics. This is the main reason people choose Waldorf and the main reason it is so successful in producing well-balanced adults. Forcing children at this developmental level to sit in desks all day completing worksheets and repeating after a teacher is clearly an incompatible educational approach, and it is possibly the main reason that traditional schools develop education gaps for lower socioeconomic groups and children who cannot sit still.

The prefrontal cortex of children at this age is still developing. Focus requires the prefrontal cortex to hold a lot of glucose which is draining. Once the pattern is recognized the basal ganglia (especially the striatum) handles future requests of the same pattern only routing to the prefrontal cortex for rule refinement. For children with a developing prefrontal cortex, pattern recognition is torturous and takes significant time. The public education institution captures the slow progress in success metrics.

I believe that the incompatible learning method may result in development of adverse epigenetic traits for children who cannot sit still or who do not have parents in the home to explain and reward their progress. Those children may develop hopelessness and anxiety regarding academic pursuits. Ironically, the pattern they may learn to recognize is that learning is harmful to them. This may lead to common utterances such as, "I just can't learn math," which follow the child through their entire academic life.

I had a successful response to this utterance while tutoring calculus. I would instruct the student to grab their textbook (or another object) in one hand and extend it creating a rough physical example of the exponential increase in leverage. We then derived on paper. This is an example of rewarding exploration with knowledge which is the explicit goal of Ant Explorer. However, thoughtful tutoring does not counter the epigenetic harm already caused by the failures of traditional education.

Waldorf claims that students will "catch up" in high school which is correct and proves that institutional rote metrics are extremely flawed. Once the prefrontal cortex is developed it is significantly easier and faster for former Waldorf students to learn the same things that were tortured into their classmates in elementary school, and the harmful development of adverse epigenetic traits is avoided. Instead Waldorf emphasizes "bilateral learning" which means students are engaged in activities that use both hemispheres of their brain. Knitting, tending a garden, stacking blocks, even simply walking outside all engage both brain hemispheres. 

Developmentally this is exactly correct for this stage because it aids in the development of the corpus callosum which connects the brain hemispheres. People with a higher level of inter-hemispheric integration exhibit higher general intelligence and abstract reasoning. So when Waldorf students arrive in traditional high school they have avoided harm, developed stronger inter-hemispheric integration, and are able to learn traditional concepts both more rapidly and with better understanding which is an excellent and proven outcome.

## On Industrialism, Automation, and Anthroposophy

Rudolf Steiner founded the first Waldorf school in 1919 industrial Germany for children of factory workers of a Waldorf-Astoria cigarette factory. His spiritual and metaphysical beliefs were strongly opposed to humanity's shift from immersion in and understanding of nature to industrial life where people are not in contact with nature, learn by rote metrics as children then serve automation on factory floors as adults. Industrial technology and automation were replacing the necessity for humans to think critically and deeply which Waldorf education directly challenges.

Waldorf is a response to these conditions, but it is also a pedagogical vehicle to indoctrinate children in his spiritual science called Anthroposophy. Anthroposophy is the belief that humans can achieve higher cosmic alignment through intellectual pursuit using imaginative visualization. I believe that what Steiner calls "clairvoyance" refers to a high capacity to produce high-fidelity imaginative visualizations which does not require accessing a spiritual realm, but is rather a conscious manifestation of the brain.

## On Abstraction, Intuitive Cognition, Default Mode Network, and Clairvoyance

I call "imaginative visualization" abstraction. I use abstraction to model system dynamics and constraints. The model allows me to infer or intuit information about things that are unseen that would have otherwise been unknowable. This is called intuitive cognition.

Abstraction uses the default mode network. The parts of the default mode network used for three-dimensional modeling comprise the medial temporal/spatial subsystem. This subsystem is commonly known as the mind's eye. The subsystem consists of the precuneus (renderer), the retrosplenial cortex (state and physics engine), the parahippocampal cortex (boundaries and constraints), hippocampus (archive), and the posterior cingulate cortex (router). The router boots, then data is pulled from the archive to derive boundaries and constraints for the physics engine to compute state for the renderer to display to the mind's eye.

It is called the "default" mode network because it was accidentally discovered in the 1990s by a neurologist who, out of boredom, observed that subjects in fMRI scanners used a different part of their brain when performing tasks vs not doing tasks. He called not doing tasks in fMRI scanners resting so he called the mode default. Thus default mode network. The subjects were likely closing their eyes daydreaming considering their environment—a form of light abstraction. 

The default mode network can also be used to model physical reality in real-time. Quarterbacks who "see" the play are running and rendering a three-dimensional simulation of it from memory and refining it against observed reality. Musicians adept at improvisation "feel" the music by creating constraints for the piece's structure, timing, and emotional arc, and allowing them to apply known or novel patterns of notes that comprise the melodies of the music they intend to play.

The study that introduced the default mode network had the epistemic integrity to state, "Any control state, no matter how carefully it is selected, is just another task state with its own unique areas of activation." So continuing to call it "default" mode is lazy on academia's part.

## On Calculators, Simulations, and Technological Enablement

Waldorf Societies maintain a reductive and lazy view on technology. All technology that helps is viewed as a crutch. Screens that a child views educational material on are called passive. Screens with interfaces a child interacts with are called games which are assumed to be commercial, shallow, and dopamine driven. Phones have app stores and notifications that children are not emotionally mature enough to be exposed to for safety. This view is extremely black and white. Technology is viewed by its outer shell which is the antithesis of Anthroposophical practice. The members of the society are more concerned with maintaining Steiner's 1920s dogma than they are with applying the underlying principles of his spiritual science which is a perfect irony.

Is a calculator a crutch?

Students used to claim, "I will always have a calculator when I need one." in math classes. Now they state, "My phone has a calculator so I will always have one." In practice when a problem requires "181 - 62" they fumble for the phone in their pocket, potentially see a notification, check the notification then forget about the calculation entirely. By using a calculator to avoid learning to do basic computation on paper or in their head the students restrain their ability to reason about their experience with numerical fidelity which lowers their cosmic alignment. This is where a calculator is a crutch.

If you stop here and claim all calculators are crutches then you are viewing the outer shell of calculation without attempting a deeper understanding.

The AI Theory course at Cal teaches derivation from constraints which is a phenomenally useful skill. I would model the constraints into simulations using the defunct language Scheme that would take minutes to run on my laptop CPU. This is simply use of a complicated calculator. Separately, I would work on the derivation with my group. When we were confident in our derivation we would check it against the simulation results. If the simulation model was high fidelity then the results could be used to submit derivation with increased confidence. Here, input to the calculator requires the derivation of a model from constraints to begin with, and derivation of the problem itself is done before comparing it to the results. The calculator in this example is not a crutch, but rather an aid that enables higher fidelity derivation which is a form of abstraction. Ultimately, this proves that proper use of calculation to aid and enable can increase cosmic alignment.

## On Generative Creation, Frances's Ant Abstraction, and Ant Explorer

Ant Explorer is not a game or even a colony simulation in the Anthroposophical sense. When a parent creates a custom educational aid to help their child better understand nature it is what Anthroposophists call generative creation.

Frances was interested in ants. At recess she would watch ants crawl out of a hole on the playground. She knew some things about ants. She observed their size and color. She gauged how many were on the surface. She observed their speed and constant work. She knew that the bigger ones were soldiers and the smaller ones were workers. She knew that there was a queen below the surface. She knew that ants built tunnels and chambers in a large and complex subterranean colony structure. She did not know everything about ants. For what she did not know she used imagination to fill the gaps.

This is an example of abstraction. While observing the ants Frances also projected her abstraction into the colony that could not be seen. Her gaps in knowledge created a lower fidelity abstraction while her cosmic alignment with ants was still much higher than her peers. When we discussed ants she would often confidently say incorrect things about them then push back when I corrected her. This pushback is a defensive reaction to my challenging her intuitive cognition built from her abstraction. Respecting that part of her abstraction is a fallacy would require her to rebuild her mental model.

Instead of pressing her on the mainly minor inconsistencies I chose to watch a documentary on ants with her so she could correct her model based on what she saw. Her watching a documentary after already deriving her ant abstraction is an enablement aid like the Scheme simulations rather than a passive activity because the documentary is viewed with engagement against her current mental model.

While watching there was a maybe two minute segment on larvae pupating to castes. The documentary stated many complicating factors then simply moved on. I had a moment of clairvoyance. I immediately saw through the veil of the short explanation to a vector space that represents larvae by age, location, temperature, nutrients, and juvenile hormones in the context of abundance and social hormone distribution. Nurses apply small changes that accumulate over time until stochastic caste capture occurs. 

I checked my derivation against current literature to find that it was high fidelity and that my system model was both novel and potentially publishable. I built software and applied it to various domains including solving a domain transfer problem that used software architecture's connascence scoring for medication change risk given multiple comorbidities in autoimmune patients. The underlying mechanics of ant larvae had given me a model that improved my ability to reason about complex problems in reality.

Frances led me to this revelation so I wanted to give it back. To do so, I created Ant Explorer. A high fidelity colony simulation constrained to about one hundred ants, one queen, and twelve chambers in a horizontal grid. I included educational video content from the same documentary in every chamber accessed by a "knowledge star" and three pheromone trails that enter the player ant into the job queue for various castes with original narration content. I loaded it onto a rooted Moto G Play 2024 creating a kiosk that eliminated all other apps and making it child-safe. 

Additionally, Waldorf- and child-friendly system invariants were enforced to create a stress-free, non-violent experience where exploration is the loop, knowledge is the reward, and the game is played at her pace (I signed the software with the last part). When she plays she is not in a lazy ant game that triggers YouTube videos. She is immersed in a constrained yet high fidelity living ant colony. The game is a highly intellectual and imaginational generative creation that enables her to improve the fidelity of her own abstraction achieving higher cosmic alignment. If she chooses to play.

## Ant Tunnels, and the Ultimate Sin

Ant tunnels are the opposite. Outwardly, it appears to be an educational gift where she is able to observe ants as they tunnel. It is a small plexiglass cage filled with faux sand and a handful of dislocated workers and soldiers. The ants must dig tunnels to reach the water. The food includes seeds which ants cannot eat, but rather the seeds sprout destroying the tunnels. The ants endlessly walk up the side plexiglass wall struggling to maintain foothold on an unnatural material until the wall curves toward the ceiling where they fall. This at least provided an opportunity to explain that ants navigate by signal and the wall had become a pheromone trail. When I observed this it reminded me of Sisyphus so I referred to the cage as Tartarus. I was not considering Anthroposophy, but Tartarus is the lowest cosmic realm; as far below Hades as the Earth is below the heavens. An apt name.

Seeing Ant Explorer as a cheap game and the ant tunnels as real observational education represents the ultimate sin of Anthroposophy: judging the outer shell without understanding the inner mechanics that comprise the magical beauty of nature. 

Ant Explorer required immense work to create a safe learning environment, and the underlying mechanics of the game are derived from the actual underlying mechanics of ant colonies. The system ensures that the simulation will not collapse during play by adjusting leaf abundance and temperature. If left plugged in and running the colony would outlive the phone hardware. 

The ant tunnels are the opposite. They are a cheap commercial product that uses unnatural materials to house a dislocated handful of doomed ants provided with food that collapses the structures they build. Its creators clearly did not even playtest the product or they would have caught the sprouting issue. Calling it Tartarus is correct. The ant tunnel product deserves the label of cosmic trash more than Steiner's consideration of other races as cosmic leftovers.
